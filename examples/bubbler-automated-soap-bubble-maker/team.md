# Team — Bubbler

Proposed in Phase 1 from the skills map, the electromechanical classification and the envelope (consumer, outdoor/portable, budget, battery, mass production). Hands-free run: the recommended roster was kept as proposed; no roles added.

| Role | Required | Why this product needs it | Owns | Deliverables at gate |
|------|----------|---------------------------|------|----------------------|
| Product lead | Required | Owns the sub-$50 / 10k-unit envelope, the three user scenarios (party, performer, first use) and the two error scenarios, the "large bubbles reliably" promise, and every decision this run records. Also carries UX: two buttons, four LEDs and the fill-and-go flow are not enough to justify a separate UX lead. | §1, §2, §8; `high_level_design.md`; `open_questions.md` | Approved scenarios; decision log with consequences (gate gap #70); toy-vs-general-product classification (open question #6) |
| Electrical / electronics lead | Required | One 2-layer PCBA (STM32C011, HX711, DRV8837, fan MOSFET, LDO), the strain-gauge signal chain that the whole differentiator rests on, battery monitoring, EMC for FCC 15B / CE. Also carries compliance for EMC and RoHS. | §4.1, §5, §6, §9 Regulatory (EMC) | Schematic and layout released; strain-gauge signal-chain bench results (noise floor vs. 5 mN target); power budget measured on EVT; FCC/CE pre-scan plan |
| Firmware lead | Required | Bare-metal superloop with the force-curve analyser, hill-climbing optimiser, cycle state machine, soft-start motor and fan ramps, battery monitor. The adaptation loop is the product. | §4.2 | Module skeletons for the nine modules; force-curve classifier validated on prototype data (success / pop / no-film / partial); FW version struct in flash (gate gap #34) |
| Mechanical / industrial design lead | Required | Electromechanical product: dip-rotate-blow wand mechanism, L-bent duct for 0.5–2 m/s laminar flow, three-part injection-moulded enclosure (base, U-rim, removable vat), wet/dry boundary at the shaft, IPX4 electronics compartment. Enclosure tooling is 8 of the 30 weeks on the critical path. | §4.5, §9 Mechanical and Environmental, §9 Manufactured Part Lead Times | Enclosure CAD ready for tooling; wand-arm and shaft drawings with bearing and seal spec; duct airflow test; strain-gauge mounting location decided (open question #1); loop material chosen (open question #4) |
| Manufacturing / NPI lead | Recommended | Mass production at 10k+ units: three moulds, strain-gauge bonding and factory calibration (tare) as a manual step, geared-motor MOQ 5k at 6–8 weeks, EVT/DVT/PVT builds, toy-safety (ASTM F963 / EN 71) if classified as a toy. | §9 BOM Cost, Assembly and Inspection Times, Schedule, DfX | Tooling quotes for base, U-rim, vat; strain-gauge bonding and calibration work instruction; DVT and PVT build plans; production test spec (tare, motor sweep, fan ramp) |

**Not on the team, and why:** App lead and Cloud / backend lead (V1 is standalone: no app, no cloud, no radio); ML / data lead (the optimiser is integer hill-climbing on five parameters, not a trained model); UX lead (merged into Product lead, see above); Security and compliance lead (no data, no attack surface; EMC/RoHS carried by Electrical, toy safety by Manufacturing / NPI).
